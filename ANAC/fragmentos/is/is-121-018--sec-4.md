SUBJ: United States Standard for Terminal Instrument Procedures (TERPS)
This order prescribes standardized methods for designing and evaluating instrument flight
procedures (IFPs) in the United States and its territories. It is to be used by all personnel
responsible for the preparation, approval, and promulgation of IFPs. These criteria are predicated
on normal aircraft operations and performance.




Rick Domingo
Executive Director, Flight Standards Service
